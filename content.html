Content Policy for DOCK OS

Effective Date: December 19, 2024

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Our Philosophy

DOCK OS is a personal organization and productivity tool. You own your content, and we respect your privacy. However, we must maintain a safe, legal platform for all users.

Acceptable Use

DOCK OS is designed for:

  • Personal notes and ideas
  • Project planning and task management
  • Creative writing and brainstorming
  • Business notes and meeting records
  • Educational content and study notes
  • Personal journals and reflections

Prohibited Content

You may not use DOCK OS to store:

Illegal Content

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — zero tolerance, reported to NCMEC
  • Content promoting terrorism or violent extremism
  • Instructions for creating weapons, explosives, or dangerous substances
  • Stolen data, hacked information, or trade secrets
  • Content that violates export control laws

Harmful Content

  • Content promoting self-harm or suicide
  • Content intended to harass, threaten, or abuse others
  • Malware, viruses, or malicious code
  • Spam or automated abuse content

Infringing Content

  • Copyrighted material you don't have rights to use
  • Unauthorized use of trademarks
  • Content that violates others' privacy rights
  • Trade secrets or confidential information belonging to others

Other Prohibited Content

  • Content created primarily to circumvent usage limits or security measures
  • Bot-generated spam or abuse
  • Content that violates our Terms of Service

Enforcement

How We Detect Violations

  • We do not actively monitor your private content
  • We may investigate if:
    • You share content publicly and it is reported
    • Automated systems detect potential CSAM
    • We receive a valid legal request
    • Abuse patterns are detected (e.g., excessive API usage)

Consequences

Violations may result in:

  1. Warning: First-time minor violations
  2. Content Removal: Specific prohibited content deleted
  3. Feature Restriction: Paid or AI features disabled temporarily
  4. Account Suspension: Temporary loss of access (7–30 days)
  5. Account Termination: Permanent ban for serious violations
  6. Legal Action: Reported to authorities for illegal content

Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was incorrect:

  • Email: contactheaideapp@gmail.com
  • Include: Your account email, date of action, and explanation
  • We review appeals within 5–7 business days

Privacy and Content Access

When We Access Your Content

We only access your content when:

  • You explicitly grant us permission (e.g., for support requests)
  • Required by valid legal process (court order, subpoena)
  • Necessary to detect illegal content (CSAM, terrorism)
  • Investigating reported violations

What We See

  • Normal operation: We do not read your notes or content
  • AI features: Content is processed temporarily and is not stored by us beyond what is needed to fulfill your request
  • Support requests: Only content you send us for troubleshooting
  • Legal requests: Only specific content identified in valid court orders

Copyright and DMCA

Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)

If you believe content in DOCK OS infringes your copyright, send a DMCA notice to: contactheaideapp@gmail.com

Your notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work
  3. Description of where the infringing content is located
  4. Your contact information (address, phone, email)
  5. A statement of good faith belief that the use is unauthorized
  6. A statement that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will: respond within 48 hours, remove the infringing content, notify the relevant user, and terminate repeat infringers.

Counter-Notice

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake, send a counter-notice to: contactheaideapp@gmail.com

Your counter-notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature
  2. Identification of the removed content and its location
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that removal was a mistake
  4. Your contact information
  5. Consent to the jurisdiction of a federal court

We will restore content within 10–14 business days unless the copyright owner files a court action.

Content Backup and Deletion

Your Responsibilities

  • Back up important content: We provide sync, but you should export critical data regularly
  • We are not a backup service: Use DOCK OS for active work, not long-term archival
  • Deletion is permanent: Once you delete content or your account, it cannot be recovered

Our Responsibilities

  • We perform automated backups for disaster recovery purposes
  • Backups are not accessible to individual users
  • Backups are deleted after 90 days
  • We make best efforts but do not guarantee data preservation

Content Ownership

  • You own your content: All content you create belongs to you
  • Limited license: You grant us permission to store and process your content solely to provide the service
  • No other use: We do not use your content for marketing, AI training, or other purposes
  • Export anytime: You can request or download your data at any time

Reporting Violations

If you discover prohibited content or behavior:

  • Email: contactheaideapp@gmail.com
  • Include: Your contact information, a description of the violation, a link or screenshot if applicable, and the date/time you discovered it

We will investigate within 24–48 hours, take appropriate action if a violation is confirmed, and notify you of the outcome where privacy permits.

Transparency

  • DMCA notices processed
