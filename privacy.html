Privacy Policy for DOCK OS

Effective Date: December 19, 2024

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Introduction

DOCK OS ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application DOCK OS (the "App").

By using DOCK OS, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address (for authentication and account management)
  • User Content: Notes, tasks, projects, pages, databases, and any other content you create or save in the App
  • Linked Content: Links and URLs you save within the App
  • AI Interactions: Prompts you submit to AI features, and chat inputs and outputs generated during those sessions
  • Voice Input: Voice recordings or voice-to-text input if you use voice features
  • Uploaded Files: Images, files, and other media you upload to the App
  • Module and Workspace Content: Content stored within any project, page, database, or module you create
  • Subscription Information: Payment-related data handled by our payment providers depending on the platform you use. For Android purchases, payments are processed through Google Play. For Windows and web purchases, payments may be processed through Stripe, which may collect your name, email address, billing details, payment method information, and transaction details. DOCK OS does not store full credit or debit card numbers directly.

Automatically Collected Information

  • App Activity: Feature usage patterns, interactions, and in-app events
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version
  • Crash and Error Logs: Crash reports and error logs (anonymized where applicable) to help diagnose and fix issues

Information from Third Parties

  • Google Play: Subscription status and purchase verification (Android)
  • Stripe: Payment processing and verification (Windows/web)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and entitlement management
  • Authentication: Email verification through Supabase Auth

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate the App and Provide Features: Store, sync, and deliver all core app functionality to you
  • AI Processing: Send relevant content to AI services to power AI-assisted features you initiate
  • Subscription Management: Process payments and manage access to paid features
  • App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs
  • Customer Support: Respond to your requests and provide assistance
  • Security and Integrity: Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and policy violations
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

AI Processing

How AI Features Work

  • When you use AI features, relevant content (such as your prompts, notes, or context) is sent to AI service providers for processing
  • AI outputs are generated responses and are not guaranteed to be accurate — you should review any AI-generated content before acting on it
  • We do not use your content to train AI models

Data Sent to AI Providers

  • Content you explicitly submit as a prompt or chat input
  • Contextual content you have in the App when using AI features (e.g., note text, project names)
  • AI providers process this data per their own API terms and generally do not retain it for training

Data Storage and Security

Where Your Data Lives

  • Primary Database: Supabase (encrypted PostgreSQL database)
  • App Platform: Lovable Cloud and related infrastructure
  • Subscription Management: RevenueCat
  • Payment Processing (Android): Google Play
  • Payment Processing (Windows/web): Stripe
  • Location: Hosted on secure cloud infrastructure

Security Measures

  • All data transmission is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS where supported
  • Database access requires authentication
  • Row-level security policies restrict data access to account owners only
  • API keys and secrets are stored securely

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Limited Sharing

We only share data with:

  1. Service Providers:
    • Supabase: Database and authentication hosting
    • Lovable Cloud: App platform and infrastructure
    • RevenueCat: Subscription and entitlement management
    • Google Play: Payment processing and subscription management (Android)
    • Stripe: Payment processing for Windows and web purchases
    • AI Providers: Processing AI feature requests (temporary, per their API terms)
  2. Legal Requirements:
    • Court orders, subpoenas, or legal processes
    • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
    • Prevention of fraud or illegal activity

Analytics

  • We collect anonymized usage statistics to improve the app
  • No personally identifiable information is included in analytics
