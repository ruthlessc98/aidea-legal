Refund Policy for DOCK OS

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

How Purchases Work

DOCK OS is available on multiple platforms, and the payment provider used depends on where you purchased your subscription:

  • Android: Purchases and subscriptions are managed through Google Play.
  • Windows / Web: Purchases and subscriptions may be managed through Stripe.

All refund requests are reviewed in accordance with applicable law, the policies of the relevant payment provider, and our refund policy below. We do not guarantee refunds in all cases.

Android Purchases — Google Play

If you purchased DOCK OS on Android, your purchase and refund are subject to Google Play's refund policies. The fastest way to request a refund is directly through Google Play:

Google Play Refund Policy:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637
  • Open the Google Play Store app
  • Go to Account → Purchase History
  • Find your DOCK OS purchase and select Request a Refund
  • Follow the on-screen instructions

Google Play's refund window and eligibility criteria apply. Refunds approved through Google Play are processed by Google and may take several business days to appear.

Windows / Web Purchases — Stripe

If you purchased DOCK OS on Windows or via the web, your payment was processed through Stripe. To request a refund for a Stripe purchase, please contact us directly:

  1. Email us at the address below
  2. Include your registered email address, the date of purchase, and a description of your issue or reason for requesting a refund
  3. We will review your request and respond within 3–5 business days

Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis in accordance with applicable law, Stripe's payment policies, and our refund policy. We do not automatically guarantee a refund upon request.

Subscription Cancellations

Canceling your subscription is not the same as requesting a refund.

  • Cancellations prevent future charges but do not refund the current billing period
  • You retain access to paid features until the end of your current paid period
  • Android: To cancel, go to Google Play → Subscriptions → DOCK OS → Cancel
  • Windows / Web: To cancel, go to your account settings or contact support
  • If your subscription is canceled, lapses, or payment fails, paid features may become locked or read-only

Free Trials

  • Free trials can be canceled at any time without charge
  • You must cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged
  • Refunds are generally not available for trial-to-paid conversions if you did not cancel in time

Partial Refunds

We do not offer pro-rated or partial refunds for mid-period cancellations or unused features. Refund eligibility is determined by the applicable payment provider's policies and applicable law.
